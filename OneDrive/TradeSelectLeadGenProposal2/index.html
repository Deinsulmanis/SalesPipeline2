How we track it

How We Track It — Simple & Transparent

Every lead is logged the moment it comes in — you always see exactly what I've brought you.

1

Every lead flows through one system

Every lead I generate flows through a dedicated intake form I manage. The moment a lead comes in, it's timestamped and logged automatically into a shared Google Sheet you can view anytime.

2

Every channel feeds the same tracker

All my channels route through this form — outreach to property managers, referral partnerships, and the traffic my SEO work drives all funnel into one tracked system. Nothing is guesswork.

3

You mark it complete, I invoice the fee

When a job closes, you mark it complete and enter the value. I invoice 7% of closed jobs only.

4

You see everything, in real time

Full transparency — you see every lead I bring and exactly what closed, in real time. No black box, no disputes.

The attribution rule: A lead counts as sourced by ScaleLab only if it comes through my dedicated intake form or a tracked referral partnership, logged with a timestamp at the time of contact. The 7% applies to those logged leads once the job closes and you're paid. Leads that reach you through your own existing channels or walk-ins are never counted — you only ever pay on work I clearly brought you.
